# Privacy Policy for Simply Big Calculator

**Last Updated:** September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Simply Big Calculator** ("the Application", "we", "our") handles user information and data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data collected or processed when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Summary of Principles

- **No User Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details (such as name, email, or phone number) to use Simply Big Calculator.
- **Local Device Storage:** All your calculation inputs, results, calculation history, and personal application preferences (theme, display style, haptic feedback) are stored exclusively and locally on your device. We do not operate remote servers that collect or store your calculations.
- **100% Offline Calculation:** Mathematical computations are processed locally on your device processor without requiring an active internet connection.

---

## 2. Information Handled by Third-Party Services

Simply Big Calculator integrates trusted third-party services provided by **Google LLC** to support advertising and optional in-app purchases. These services may collect certain device-level information as detailed below.

### A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

The free version of Simply Big Calculator displays non-intrusive banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

- **Information Collected by Google AdMob:**
  - Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID).
  - IP address (used for approximate geographic location to serve relevant ads).
  - Ad interaction and diagnostic performance metrics (such as ad impressions and clicks).

- **Consent and Privacy Choices (GDPR / EEA / UK / applicable jurisdictions):**
  - Simply Big Calculator does not display the advertising consent form immediately when the Application is first opened.
  - In applicable jurisdictions, when advertising is first required, the Application may present Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before requesting an advertisement.
  - Users can subsequently review or modify their available privacy choices through **Privacy Options** in the Application settings, when required or made available by the applicable consent framework.

- **More Information:** You can review Google's advertising privacy practices at Google Privacy & Terms:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

### B. Google Play In-App Billing

If you purchase the optional **Simply Big Calculator Premium** to permanently remove ads, transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing.

- **Financial Information:** We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses. Payment processing is handled by Google Play and is subject to Google Play's applicable terms.
- **Entitlement Verification:** The Application receives purchase information from Google Play services as necessary to confirm your Premium ad-free status and allow eligible purchases to be restored across devices associated with the same Google account.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

Simply Big Calculator requests only the permissions necessary for its intended functionality:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required for Google AdMob to load banner advertisements and for network-based Google services used by the Application. |
| `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Allows relevant services to detect network availability. |
| `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Provides optional haptic tactile feedback when tapping calculator keys (can be disabled in Settings). |
| `com.android.vending.BILLING` | Enables secure in-app purchases for the Premium upgrade via Google Play. |
| `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` | Allows supported advertising functionality involving the Android advertising identifier where permitted. |

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

- Because your calculation history and settings are stored locally on your device, you maintain control over this locally stored data.
- You can permanently delete your calculation history at any time using the **Clear History** function within the Application.
- Uninstalling the Application or clearing its application data through Android system settings removes locally stored Application data from that device.

---

## 5. Target Audience and Children's Privacy

Simply Big Calculator is designed as a general-purpose mathematical utility for **users aged 13 and older**, including students, professionals, and adults.

- **No Age Verification Screen:** Simply Big Calculator does not require or collect your birth date, age, or age range when the Application starts.
- **Children Under 13:** Simply Big Calculator is not directed to children under 13 years of age.
- **Privacy for Users:** Mathematical calculations are performed locally on the device and the Application does not maintain proprietary remote user accounts.
- Where applicable, users can manage available advertising privacy choices through **Privacy Options** in the Application settings.
